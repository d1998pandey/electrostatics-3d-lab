1. Explore the Metal — What is a Free Electron?
In a metal, some electrons are not tightly bound to individual atoms. These conduction electrons can move through the material. The positive ions remain approximately fixed at their lattice positions.
Scientific Note: Electron motion shown here is a slowed-down pedagogical illustration of random thermal motion and average drift, not every microscopic collision.
